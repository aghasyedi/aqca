3. Run The Experiment

Alice and Bob pick their setting independently and record ±1. Repeating for the four setting pairs gives the CHSH combination S = E(a,b) − E(a,b′) + E(a′,b) + E(a′,b′).

Any local hidden-variable theory obeys |S| ≤ 2. Quantum mechanics allows up to 2√2 ≈ 2.828 — the Tsirelson bound. Crossing 2 rules out local realism.
