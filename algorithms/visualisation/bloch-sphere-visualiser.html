Bloch Sphere Visualiser

Put a single qubit anywhere on the sphere and apply X, Y, Z, H, S, T, Rx, Ry and Rz. Every gate animates as a genuine rotation of the state vector, and the amplitudes, Bloch angles and expectation values update live.

2. Fixed Gates

Each gate is a rotation of the Bloch vector: X, Y, Z are π turns about the corresponding axis, H is a π turn about (x̂+ẑ)/√2, S is a π/2 turn about ẑ and T is a π/4 turn about ẑ.

3. Rotation Gates

90°

Rx, Ry, Rz rotate the state about the x̂, ŷ, ẑ axes by the chosen angle. Try Rz(90°) then Ry(90°) and watch the vector trace a path on the sphere.
